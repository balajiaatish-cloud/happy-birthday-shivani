To Shivani on her 19th Birthday

Dearest Shivani,

Happy 19th Birthday! As you blow out the nineteen candles today, I wanted to pause and remind you how truly magnificent you are.

It's almost surreal to think that we've known each other for just one year. Sometimes people spend an entire lifetime searching for a friend who understands them without explanations, who laughs at the exact same foolish things, and who stands steady like an anchor when life gets overwhelming. In just 365 days, you became that person for me.

Thank you for every late-night conversation, every piece of advice, every shared silence, and every moment where you reminded me that genuine kindness still exists in this world. Your empathy, your brilliant smile, and your resilient spirit inspire me more than you know.

Entering 19 is a magical chapter — standing between the sweetness of youth and the limitless horizons of adulthood. May this year shower you with triumphs, unforgettable adventures, pure happiness, and peace of mind. Whatever challenges come, remember you always have a friend who believes in you relentlessly.

With endless love & gratitude,
Your Special Close Friend 🌸
