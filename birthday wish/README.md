A web experience crafted for **Shivani** on her **19th Birthday**, celebrating **1 year of close friendship**.

## 🌟 Highlights & Key Features

### 1. 🎂 Interactive 3D World (Three.js & WebGL)
- **3D Tiered Birthday Cake**: Realistic multi-tiered cake decorated with pearl trim, dripping frosting, and 19 mini candles representing each of her 19 years.
- **Golden "19" 3D Topper**: Metallic 3D numerals reflecting warm stage lighting and stardust.
- **Interactive Candle Blowout**:
  - Tap the cake or click **"Blow Candle"** to extinguish the flame.
  - Realistic smoke particles drift up as golden confetti and fireworks burst across the screen!
  - You can also enable **"Mic Blow"** to physically blow into your phone or laptop microphone!
  - Click **"Relight Candle"** to make another wish anytime.
- **3D Floating Metallic Balloons**:
  - Floating balloons with realistic physics strings bobbing in the breeze.
  - **Interactive Pop**: Tap any balloon to pop it with particle effects and sound, revealing a customized compliment / sweet note for Shivani!
- **3D Floating Gift Box**:
  - Royal violet and gold ribboned gift box.
  - Click the box to watch the lid fly open with magical sparkles, unlocking the secret handwritten letter.
- **3D Sky Lanterns (Wishes)**:
  - Tap **"Release Lantern"** to send glowing sky lanterns ascending into the starry night sky.
- **3D Stardust Universe & Lighting**:
  - Over 1,200 floating stardust and firefly particles drifting in a cosmic starry night.
  - Smooth camera controls (drag/swipe to rotate, pinch to zoom, auto-rotate on idle).

## 🚀 How to Open & View

### Option 1: Direct Double-Click
Simply double-click `index.html` in your file explorer. It will open in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari, or Firefox.

### Option 2: Run a Local Server (Recommended for Mobile Sharing)
Open PowerShell or Terminal in this folder (`d:\birthday wish`) and run:

```bash
# Using Python
python -m http.server 8080

# OR using Node
npx serve
```
Then:
- **On your laptop**: open `http://localhost:8080`
- **On your phone**: connect to the same Wi-Fi and open `http://<YOUR_LAPTOP_IP>:8080` (e.g. `http://192.168.1.5:8080`).

### Option 3: Host Online for Free (To Send the Link to Shivani)
1. **GitHub Pages**: Push this folder to a GitHub repository and enable GitHub Pages in Settings.
2. **Netlify / Vercel**: Drag and drop this folder onto [netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) to get a free live URL (e.g., `https://shivani-19th-birthday.netlify.app`) in 10 seconds!
